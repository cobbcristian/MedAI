## MedAI
**AI-assisted operations for telemedicine & ambulatory care**  
Confidential · [Date] · [Your name]

---

## Problem
- After-visit documentation and inbox work consume clinician capacity.
- Virtual care programs bottleneck on **intake, follow-up, and measurable ops**—not ads alone.
- Systems need **guardrailed AI**: assistive, auditable, governed by clinical leadership.

---

## Thesis
**Shorten time to a signed, compliant chart**—without replacing licensed clinical judgment.

> MedAI is a **workflow & documentation layer** for your medical group or telehealth operator—not autonomous diagnosis or prescribing.

---

## Product (today)
| Area | What ships |
|------|------------|
| Core | FastAPI backend, React app, auth, chat, video hooks |
| Clinical assist | Doctor Copilot: SOAP / visit summaries for **clinician approval** |
| Artifacts | Broad **lab** (PDF, CSV, TSV, XLS, XLSX) & **imaging** upload formats |
| Ops | Pilot metrics log, CRM/leads, outreach email (e.g. Resend) when configured |

---

## Who buys / pilots
- Telehealth MSOs, multi-specialty groups, digital health operators  
- Programs: chronic / metabolic care, virtual-first clinics, screening-adjacent education (with counsel)

---

## Why now
- Payers and operators are adopting **AI for admin burden** under higher scrutiny.  
- Winners pair **workflow metrics** (time-to-note, edit rate, NPS) with **governance**.
